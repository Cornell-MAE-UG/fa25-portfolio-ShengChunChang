I selected Problem Set 5 for my portfolio because it provides a highly practical framework for analyzing thermal management systems, which is a critical aspect of mechanical engineering and hardware design. For the first problem, I explored fin arrays and thermal resistance networks by studying heat sinks. In the second problem, I analyzed the transient heating of a wire submerged in an oil bath. These problems are useful to me as I integrate heat sinks onto electronic speed controllers in custom drone builds. By breaking down the contact resistance and conduction through the base, I gained a clearer understanding of how to optimize heat dissipation for power-dense flight electronics.
