  <p style="clear: both;"><strong>Falco V1 (In Progress): </strong> Falco V1 is a stealth-inspired UAV concept focused on aerodynamic integration, propulsion packaging, and modular payload architecture. The design blends the wing, fuselage, and nacelle/duct surfaces into a continuous form to reduce sharp transitions and improve structural continuity.</p>
</div>

<div>
  <p><strong>Propulsion Layout (dual lift + rear pusher): </strong> The front section uses two large lift propeller modules integrated into protective duct housings to improve clearance management and provide a rigid mounting structure. A rear pusher propulsor is placed along the centerline to provide forward thrust for cruise, enabling smoother hover-to-forward transition and reducing the need for aggressive vehicle pitch during forward flight.</p>
</div>

<div>
  <p><strong>Modular Rotary Payload Bay (concept): </strong> The central cylindrical bay is designed as a modular internal payload space for swappable non-weapon modules such as sensor packages, mapping hardware, or small supply pods. A rotary carousel concept is being explored to index modules while keeping mass close to the centerline to reduce CG shift and simplify balance across configurations.</p>
</div>

<div>
  <p><strong>Engineering Focus: </strong> This concept is being developed as a CAD-first systems layout exercise. Next iterations will define a mass budget and thrust-to-weight targets, add internal ribs/bosses for manufacturable part breakdown, and run preliminary structural checks on the duct mounts and wing-root regions.</p>
</div>

<div>
  <p><strong>Currently Working on: </strong> (1) Mass and thrust sizing for hover and climb. (2) Internal structure and fastener strategy. (3) First-pass FEA on high-load regions. (4) Split into printable or CNC-friendly components. (5) Drawing package with key dimensions and assembly notes.</p>
</div>
